Tipper Truck Hiring Policy.

The standard terms, rates and conditions under which PLMB Engineering Investments Limited hires out its tipper truck and operator services.

Policy No.PLMB/HR-POL/TIPPER/01
Effective Date26 August 2026
Approved ByPLMB Engineering Investments Limited Management
Review CycleAnnually, or as market conditions require

1. Purpose

This policy sets out the standard terms, rates, and conditions under which PLMB Engineering Investments Limited ("PLMB", "the Company") hires out its tipper truck(s) and operator services to clients ("the Customer", "the Hirer"). It is intended to ensure consistency, transparency, and fairness in all tipper hire arrangements entered into by the Company.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all tipper truck hiring engagements undertaken by PLMB Engineering Investments Limited, whether for construction, earthworks, haulage, or other material-transport purposes, and covers both short-term (daily) and longer-term hire arrangements.

3. Equipment and Operator

Each hire includes the tipper truck in good, roadworthy mechanical condition together with a competent, licensed operator provided by the Company. The Customer does not provide a driver/operator unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4. Hiring Rates and Charges

  • Daily Hiring Rate: MK450,000.00 (Four Hundred and Fifty Thousand Malawi Kwacha) per day, covering eight (8) working hours.
  • Operator Allowance: MK30,000.00 (Thirty Thousand Malawi Kwacha) per day, payable to cover the operator's subsistence for the duration of the hire.
  • Fuel: The Customer shall be solely responsible for supplying fuel for the tipper truck for the entire duration of the hire. PLMB will not be liable for any delays or work stoppages arising from the Customer's failure to provide fuel.

All rates quoted are exclusive of any applicable taxes and levies unless expressly stated otherwise in the hire agreement or invoice.

5. Working Hours and Overtime

The standard working day is eight (8) hours, typically 07:00 to 16:00 with a one-hour break, or as otherwise agreed with the Customer in advance.

  • Any hours worked beyond the standard 8-hour day shall be billed as overtime at a pro-rata hourly rate based on the Daily Hiring Rate (MK450,000.00 ÷ 8 hours = MK56,250.00 per hour).
  • Work requested on Sundays or public holidays shall attract a premium rate, to be agreed with the Customer prior to mobilisation.
  • Standby or idle time caused by the Customer (e.g. site not ready, no loading/offloading point available) will still be charged at the full Daily Hiring Rate.

6. Minimum Hire Period

The minimum hire period is one (1) full working day. Part-day bookings will be charged at the full Daily Hiring Rate unless otherwise agreed in writing.

7. Booking and Confirmation

  • Hire requests should be made at least 24 hours in advance to allow for scheduling and mobilisation, though shorter-notice requests will be accommodated where the tipper is available.
  • A booking is only confirmed once the Customer has agreed to the applicable rates and, where required, made payment as set out in Section 8.
  • The Customer must provide clear site access, loading/offloading instructions, and a site contact person prior to mobilisation.

8. Payment Terms

  • New and one-off clients: full payment is due prior to mobilisation of the tipper truck, unless otherwise agreed with Management.
  • Established/corporate clients on account: payment is due within seven (7) days of invoice date, unless a separate credit agreement specifies otherwise.
  • Payments may be made by bank transfer, mobile money, or cash against an official PLMB receipt/invoice.
  • Extended hires (multiple days) may be invoiced daily, weekly, or on completion, as agreed at the time of booking.

9. Customer Responsibilities

  • Supplying fuel for the tipper truck throughout the hire period.
  • Providing safe, accessible loading and offloading points.
  • Ensuring the material to be transported is suitable and lawful for haulage by tipper truck.
  • Providing loading labour/equipment unless separately agreed with PLMB.
  • Promptly notifying PLMB of any changes to the scope, site, or schedule of works.

10. PLMB's Responsibilities

  • Providing a roadworthy, serviced tipper truck fit for the agreed purpose.
  • Providing a competent, licensed, and properly conducted operator.
  • Maintaining valid insurance, road licences, and fitness certification for the vehicle.
  • Carrying out routine servicing and maintenance of the tipper truck at the Company's own cost.

11. Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled hire date will not attract a charge.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled hire date, or after the tipper has been mobilised to site, may attract a cancellation charge of up to 50% of the Daily Hiring Rate to cover mobilisation costs.

12. Liability, Damages and Insurance

The tipper truck and its operator remain under the Company's operational control at all times during the hire. PLMB shall not be liable for delays, losses, or damages arising from circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to poor site access, unsuitable loading conditions, or Customer-caused delays. The Customer shall be liable for any loss or damage to the tipper truck arising from misuse, unsafe site conditions, or instructions given by the Customer that are inconsistent with the safe operation of the vehicle.

13. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be held liable for any failure or delay in performance under this policy resulting from circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to natural disasters, civil unrest, government action, or breakdown of essential infrastructure.

14. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising from a tipper hire arrangement shall first be referred to PLMB Management for amicable resolution. Should the matter remain unresolved, it shall be handled in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Malawi.

15. Amendment of This Policy

This policy may be reviewed and amended from time to time at the discretion of PLMB Engineering Investments Limited Management to reflect changes in operating costs, fuel prices, or market conditions. The rates in effect at the time of booking confirmation shall apply for that hire.
