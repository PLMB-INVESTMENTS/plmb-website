Our Story

From a sole trader to a limited company.

Founded in Blantyre in 2012 as P.L.M.B Civil Engineering, the business widened into building, water and irrigation works in 2017, and incorporated as PLMB Engineering Investments Limited in November 2025, a structure built for the scale its founders intend to reach.

  1. 2012Founded as P.L.M.B Civil Engineering, a sole-trading construction outfit in Blantyre.
  2. 2017Victor Chabwera joins as partner; renamed PLMB Engineering Investments, widening into building, water and irrigation works.
  3. 2018VAT registered with the Malawi Revenue Authority; head office established in Blantyre.
  4. 2020–24Delivers major contracts for Central East African Railways, WaterAid Malawi, the Malawi Legal Aid Bureau, Gratitude Hostels and Nkhotakota District Council.
  5. 2024Joint venture with Destiny Construction to deliver staff housing for the Malawi Police Service.
  6. 2025–26Achieves NCIC Class 5 in Building and Civil; incorporates as PLMB Engineering Investments Limited; takes over the completed Kaombe Primary School contract.
